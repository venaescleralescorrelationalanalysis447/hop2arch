# Code of Conduct

Be decent to people. Argue about the entry, not about the person who wrote it.

Two rules are specific to this project, and they are the ones actually worth writing down.

**Do not make anyone feel stupid for using Windows.** Everyone reading a hop report is doing
something that took nerve. Treating Windows, its users, or the reasons somebody stayed on it as a
punchline is not welcome here. Neither is the reverse.

**Do not argue by confidence.** "It works fine for me" is not an answer to "it broke for me". Say
what you tested and where. `confidence = "low"` exists so that nobody has to pretend to be sure.

Not acceptable: harassment, insults, personal attacks, discrimination on any basis, publishing
someone's private information — including anything extracted from a hopfile attached to an issue —
or continuing unwanted attention after being asked to stop.

Someone who attaches an unscrubbed hopfile has made a mistake, not committed an offence. Help them
replace it; do not comment on the contents.

This applies to issues, pull requests, commit messages, code review, and anywhere someone is
representing the project.

Report a problem through
[GitHub private reporting](https://github.com/Ramirmir/hop2arch/security/advisories/new), or open an
issue if it is not sensitive. One maintainer, so expect a reply in days. The first outcome is a
conversation; repeated or serious behaviour gets the account blocked from the repository.

Adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/) 2.1, with the parts
that only make sense for larger projects left out rather than kept as decoration.
